Getting
there.

Having a car in the region is a necessity, but there is a bus from NYC to Monticello (20 min away from us) if you will be coming up from the city and meeting friends.

Drive yourself

We'll provide parking near our house — details to come. Please carpool if you can; parking is limited.

  • From NYC ~2 hrs
  • Closest town Monticello

Saturday school bus

We've arranged a large school bus shuttle to transport guests to and from the celebration. It'll make two trips, departing first from The North Branch Inn around 3 PM, with planned stops at pre-approved Airbnbs along the way. If you'd like to take advantage of the shuttle and are planning to stay in a rented house, please reach out so we can help coordinate. Return trips between 10 PM and 2 AM.

  • Depart ~3:00 PM
  • Returns 10 PM – 2 AM

NYC → Monticello

Shortline / Coach USA runs from Port Authority to Monticello, 20 minutes from the house. Good if you're meeting friends with a car at the other end.

  • Ride time ~2.5 hrs
  • Frequency Multiple / day

Where
to sleep.

We've reserved a room block at The North Branch Inn — the Friday welcome dinner is there, and the Saturday shuttle is guaranteed to stop. Book early.

The North Branch Inn

If you would like to book a room at The North Branch Inn, you can use this link to book our room block. Check out their website here — it's a really charming place surrounded by a creek and gardens. It is owned and operated by the former owners of the beloved Brooklyn restaurant Le Fond. This is where we will have the welcome drinks and where the shuttle will be guaranteed to pickup and drop off on Saturday night.

Airbnb in the area

If you'd like to stay in an Airbnb and utilize the shuttle, please reach out to us to confirm the shuttle will be able to stop at your location. (thank you!)

Local inns & B&Bs

If neither of these options feel quite right for you, there are a couple of other local inns and B&Bs. Feel free to search around Bethel, NY to see what's available!

Things to do around town

Eat & Drink
  • Jeffersonville Bakeshop Local coffee + afternoon sweet treat, seating to work
  • Old Foundation Wood fire pizza and Italian fare
  • Callicoon Wine Merchant Intimate spot, small bites, wine/beer
  • Seminary Hill Cider Gorgeous view, great spot for lunch
Shop
  • Sullivan Sundries Great coffee, cheeses, wine
  • Shopping on Main St. Local antique and thrift stores in Callicoon or Jeffersonville
  • Sunday Farmers Market Local farms and food, 9am–2pm
Do
  • Yoga at Kenoza Hall Check their schedule — yoga classes and bar
  • Tusten Mountain Trail Great hike with views over the Delaware River
  • Crystal Lake Swimming
  • Skinner's Falls Tubing and swimming on the Delaware
  • Upward Brewing Beautiful setting with an adjacent fun short hike
